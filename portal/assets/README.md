# Thumbnail source and provenance

`thumbnail-source-v3.png` is the selected editable source from a two-step OpenAI image-generation flow on 2026-08-01. Its embedded C2PA manifest identifies trained algorithmic media. `thumbnail-v3.png` is the exact 256×256 `sips` derivative selected for the Mod Portal and copied byte-for-byte to the mod root as `thumbnail.png`.

`ai-assistance-source-v1.png` preserves the first generic circuit-brain generation source; its superseded 256×256 preview was exactly reproducible and is intentionally not retained. `ai-assistance-source-v2.png` preserves the first Claude-referencing revision with its dark square backdrop and is the exact artifact named by the recorded C2PA audit. `ai-assistance-source-v3.png` is the selected chroma-key generation source, and `ai-assistance-v3.png` is its full-resolution transparent master produced with the repository-recorded matte/despill settings. The exact transparent 256×256 runtime derivative is `graphics/technology/ai-assistance.png`. Factorio 2.1.12's base technology art uses 256×256 sources and its core technology slot is 72 pixels wide. Reviews against light and red technology-slot backgrounds, at 72×72, and at the reused 40×40 item-slot size confirmed that the silhouette and orange display mark remain distinct without a square backing plate.

The selected chroma-key source's embedded C2PA 2.2 manifest records `OpenAI Media Service API`, `gpt-image` 2.0, and the `trainedAlgorithmicMedia` digital source type. `c2patool` 0.27.3 found no asset-integrity validation error on the earlier audited source, but did report `signingCredential.untrusted` with its default trust configuration; this repository therefore records the embedded provenance claim without claiming that the signer is trusted. Local transparency and resize derivatives contain no C2PA manifest, as recorded in `manifest.json`.
